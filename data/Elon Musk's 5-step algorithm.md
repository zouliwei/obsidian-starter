The 5 steps of Elon Musk's engineering and production algorithm:
1. **Make your requirements less dumb**: question every requirement, especially ones from smart people. Each requirement should have a named owner, not a department.
2. **Delete the part or process**: remove as much as possible. If you aren't adding back at least 10% later, you didn't delete enough.
3. **Simplify or optimize**: only after steps 1 and 2. The classic mistake is optimizing something that shouldn't exist.
4. **Accelerate cycle time**: speed up, but only after the first three steps.
5. **Automate**: last.

The trap is doing these in reverse: automating and optimizing a step before realizing it should have been deleted.
