### 1. Service interval

BMW's recommended schedule:

- First service at 1,000 km (600 miles): change the break-in oil.
- Then every year or every 10,000 km (6,000 miles).

### 2. Core maintenance

- Change the oil & filter: every year.
- Service the drive chain (clean, lube & adjust): every 300 miles.
- Inspect brake pads & fluids: every 2 years.
- Air filter: every 20,000 km.
- Adjust tire pressures
- Inspect suspension
- Inspect coolant level & condition
- Adjust cables and tighten chassis bolts

### 3. Where to service

- **Independent shop**: usually cheaper; good for routine service after warranty.
- **Authorized dealer**: certified techs and factory diagnostics; best during warranty and for firmware updates.
