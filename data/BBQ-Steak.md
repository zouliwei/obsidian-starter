## Ingredients
- Beef Steak
- Worcestershire Sauce
- Prime Beef Rub
- Steak Rub
- Butter
## Operations
1. **Trim**: pick a well-marbled steak and trim loose fat so it cooks evenly.
2. **First seasoning**: brush on a thin layer of Worcestershire as a binder, press on Prime Beef Rub (sides too), and rest 30-45 minutes at room temperature.
3. **Two-zone fire**: bank lit charcoal on one side; preheat the grate to 550-600°F with the lid closed.
4. **Second seasoning & butter**: right before grilling, press on coarse Steak Rub for crust. Mix softened butter with Steak Rub and a few drops of Worcestershire.
5. **Sear** over direct heat, lid closed:
    - 2 min, rotate 45°, 2 min (crosshatch marks);
    - flip, 2 min, rotate 45°, 2 min.
6. **Finish & rest**: move to indirect heat until the center hits 130°F (medium rare). Rest on the butter for 7-10 minutes.
## Technique
- **Two-zone fire**: direct heat for crust, indirect heat to cook through without flare-ups.
- **Layered seasoning**: fine rub first for flavor, coarse rub last for texture.
- **Butter rest**: juices and melted butter make an instant sauce.
