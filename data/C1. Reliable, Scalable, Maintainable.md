Chapter 1 of *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* - [[DDIA]].

### Reliability

The system keeps working correctly when things go wrong (hardware faults, software bugs, human error). It is **fault-tolerant**, so faults don't become **failures** users can see.
- **Availability**: the share of time the system is up.


### Scalability

The system keeps its performance as **load parameters** grow (data volume, traffic, complexity) by adding **resources**.
- **Scaling up** (vertical): a more powerful machine
- **Scaling out** (horizontal): more machines
- **Elastic**: scales automatically


### Maintainability

The system is **easy** to operate, understand and change over time.
- **Operability**: easy day-to-day operations (monitoring, debugging, rollouts).
- **Simplicity**: manage **complexity** with good **abstractions**.
- **Evolvability**: easy to change, also called **extensibility**.
