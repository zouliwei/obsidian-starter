### Amex $200 airline fee credit: small-ticket trick

- The credit resets every **calendar year**.
- Select the airline (e.g. Southwest) **at least a day early**; the change takes time to apply.
- Buy a small fare of **about $100 or less**.
- A **refundable** fare cancelled after the credit posts goes back to the original card.
- A **non-refundable** fare cancelled after the credit posts becomes airline travel credit.
