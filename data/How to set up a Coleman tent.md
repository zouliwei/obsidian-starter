1. Clear the site and lay down the footprint.
2. Spread the tent body on top, door facing away from the wind.
3. Assemble the poles and slide them through the sleeves, crossing in an X.
4. Bend the poles into the corner pins to raise the tent.
5. Attach the rainfly, then stake out corners and guy lines.
